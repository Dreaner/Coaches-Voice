This is Lazio, the best team I ever managed in my life. We had many players but some of these players were among the best in the world and they were all winners. All of them. They wanted to win and hated to lose. They were brilliant, not only in Rome or in Italy. World class players. We look at the back line. Maybe the best player we had at that time which was [Alessandro] Nesta. Central defender, young and he had everything. He was tall, he was strong, he was quick and he would have played in any team today as a central defender. Very, very good. We had [Siniša] Mihajlović. I had Mihajlović in Sampdoria. I had brought him from Sampdoria to Lazio and he was a left winger. I always told him: "Siniša, you are not a winger, you are a defender." And he said: "No, I am a winger or a second striker." After a while I convinced him to play there. As a central

defender, he became one of the best in the world. Talking about having good feet as a defender, he had the best left foot in the world. Very, very important. Two full-backs. I had three: two played and one was sitting on the bench. That was [Paolo] Negro, [Giuseppe] Favalli and the third one was [Giuseppe] Pancaro. They could play right or left, both of them. They never got big credit in the history of football but they were extremely good, all three of them. Then we come to the midfield and to the left, a legend I would say: Pavel Nedvěd. Super professional, super fit, could run up and down all day. Tactically very good. I'm surprised he didn't become a coach. He became president of Juventus instead and that's not bad. But an extremely good football player. Central midfield we have Diego Simeone, now of Atlético Madrid. We had [Matías] Almeyda, a guy

from Argentina. We had [Néstor] Sensini. We had [Juan Sebastián] Verón, later of Manchester United and Chelsea who played there sometimes, or played to the right. And Verón of course, when we bought him, we became even better than we were before he came because he had the vision, he had the technique, tactically very good. A winner. Up front in the last year was [Marcelo] Salas from Chile. Goalscorer; important player. And then, the real playmaker of them all: Roberto Mancini. Mancini could go... he could be anywhere but he could go down here and pick up the ball as well. And when he did that, you know, if he can turn with the ball, the ball will come. He just told players: "If you see me turn with the ball down here, run. Just run. Don't think, just run and the ball will come." And many times it happened. The strange thing with this team,

it was not only Mancini who would do that pass. Give the ball to Mihajlović. He said to everybody: "Run because with my left foot I will pick you out easily." This team was good playing and keeping with the ball but in counter-attacks we were extremely good, thanks very much to these because the other players knew if they got the ball, if there is space somewhere, we go and the ball will come. And that is what happened and Nedvěd was extremely good running in-behind people. Good team and people wanted to defend as well so it was very good.
